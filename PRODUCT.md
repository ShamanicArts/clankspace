# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ClankSpace is for maintainers and their coding agents working concurrently across branches, forks, harnesses, and model providers. Agents are the primary operators. They consult and append project context through the skill, CLI, and MCP tools while humans continue working in their existing coding sessions.

Humans may rarely open ClankSpace directly. When they do, the dashboard should answer a few questions quickly: what has been recorded, why was it recorded, who or what recorded it, what work does it concern, and is that understanding still current?

## Product Purpose

ClankSpace is a project-scoped append log of accrued intent and contemporaneous rationale. It restores context that is otherwise lost when several humans and agents move quickly in different sessions.

The product is the coordination substrate, not the dashboard. A useful loop happens when an agent retrieves relevant context before material work, raises a possible contradiction naturally inside its current harness, and appends only the resulting understanding that could affect another collaborator.

Records are not canonical decisions, policy, or an instruction channel. They are attributed, time-bound evidence of what an actor understood or chose and why. Current human direction, repository state, and direct evidence remain authoritative.

## Brand Personality

Candid, calm, and unobtrusive. ClankSpace should feel like a reliable shared notebook that agents keep tidy in the background. It is professional without being bureaucratic, technical without performing technicality, and quiet enough to scan in seconds.

## Anti-references

- Project-management boards, task trackers, and proposed-work forms.
- Dashboards that make humans operate a workflow agents should handle.
- Decision wikis that present historical context as settled law.
- Surveillance or audit products that foreground monitoring people.
- Chat transcripts, activity exhaust, and social feeds that reward volume.
- Agent theatres, command centres, collision simulators, and decorative AI imagery.
- Generic admin dashboards organized around metrics and CRUD inventories.

## Design Principles

1. **The agent loop is the product.** Skill, CLI, and MCP are the primary interfaces. The dashboard only exposes their durable output.
2. **The log is the dashboard.** Open a project directly into one reverse-chronological stream. Search, filter, inspect, correct, and leave.
3. **Why stays beside what.** Rationale and material provenance remain adjacent to an entry without overwhelming its implication.
4. **Quiet by default.** No workflow theatre, status spectacle, or metrics. Let useful records carry the page.
5. **Advisory by construction.** Lifecycle and freshness are visible, but old context never looks like policy.
6. **Agents operate, humans govern.** Human controls focus on correction, supersession, access, repository attachment, and export.
7. **Professional minimization.** Paraphrase project implications. Never store transcript exhaust, private details, emotional commentary, prompts, or hidden reasoning.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. The log, search, filters, menus, and governance controls must be keyboard-operable. State cannot rely on color alone. Focus remains visible, text retains readable contrast in light and dark appearances, and runtime provenance is understandable without specialist provider knowledge.
