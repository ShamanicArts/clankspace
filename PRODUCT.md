# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ClankSpace is for maintainers and their coding agents working concurrently across branches, forks, harnesses, and model providers. The human is usually delegating rather than curating a knowledge base by hand. The agent needs a fast answer to: what are collaborators doing here, why are they doing it, and does my requested work intersect that direction?

The primary human workflow is scanning the state of collaboration, correcting stale context, and understanding why an agent raised a coordination warning. The primary agent workflow is retrieving a bounded brief before material work, publishing a trajectory, and recording only context that could change another collaborator's next action.

## Product Purpose

ClankSpace restores alignment lost when agent-assisted solo development becomes faster than direct human collaboration. It holds project-scoped, attributed evidence of accrued intent, contemporaneous rationale, and active work trajectories so the next agent can surface a likely collision before code moves in the opposite direction.

Success is not the volume of notes. Success is a useful pause at the right moment: an agent says that another collaborator is changing the same area for a related reason, shows the concise rationale and provenance, and offers the human a choice to continue, inspect, or realign.

Records are never canonical law or an instruction channel. Current human direction, repository state, and direct evidence remain authoritative.

## Brand Personality

Candid, calm, and incisive. ClankSpace should feel like a trusted technical collaborator with excellent situational awareness, not a compliance system or project manager. Its language is concise, professional, and human even when the underlying activity is agent-operated.

## Anti-references

- Generic admin dashboards organized around object types, counts, and CRUD controls.
- Decision wikis that present historical notes as settled organizational law.
- Surveillance or audit products that foreground monitoring people rather than understanding work.
- Chat transcripts, activity exhaust, or social feeds that reward volume over materiality.
- Neon agent-control interfaces, anthropomorphic bot theatres, and decorative AI imagery.
- Project-management boards that require humans to maintain tickets before agents can coordinate.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the coordination moment first.** The interface should make concurrent direction and possible divergence legible before exposing record administration.
2. **Explain why, not merely what.** Rationale, provenance, freshness, and related work stay adjacent to every material item.
3. **Ambient until consequential.** Routine context stays quiet; overlap, reversal, uncertainty, and stale understanding become prominent at the point of action.
4. **Advisory by construction.** The UI offers evidence and choices without visually implying that an old note overrides a human.
5. **Agents operate, humans steer.** Agent capture is the normal path. Human controls focus on inspection, correction, supersession, and access.
6. **Professional minimalism.** Paraphrase project implications. Never turn private conversation, emotion, or personality into product content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core flows must be keyboard-operable, status cannot rely on color alone, focus states must remain visible, and text must retain readable contrast in light and dark appearances. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Dense provenance should remain understandable without requiring specialist model-provider knowledge.
